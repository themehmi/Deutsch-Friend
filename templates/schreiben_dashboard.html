📖 Deutsch Friend Guide

Welcome to Deutsch Friend!

Your ultimate AI companion for mastering the German language. Here's what you can do:

📚 Grammatik (Grammar)

Learn cases, reflexive verbs, and fixed prepositions. Practice with the built-in Verb Conjugation engine, or chat with the AI Grammar Tutor to ask any German-related questions!

🤖 AI Grammar Tutor & Dictionary

Have a question? Ask the dedicated Grammar Tutor chatbot on the Grammar page. Or, use the floating Dictionary widget (bottom right) from any page to instantly translate words and conjugate verbs.

📖 Lesen (Reading)

Read authentic German texts customized to your level (A1-B2) and answer comprehension questions.

🎧 Hören (Listening)

Listen to generated audio scenarios. Type out the transcript and let the AI grade your accuracy.

🗣️ Sprechen (Speaking)

Record your voice to practice speaking. The AI will analyze your pronunciation and grammar in real-time.

✍️ Schreiben (Writing)

Write emails or essays based on AI prompts and receive instant grammar corrections and a CEFR level.

Full App Tutorial

Step 1: Set Up Your Account

Start by clicking "Sign Up" in the top left menu. Creating an account allows the app to securely save your preferences (like your preferred learning level) directly to the cloud. Once logged in, you'll see your profile icon in the top left corner!

Step 2: Navigation & The Sidebar Arrow

Look for the ▶ or ◀ arrow on the left side of the screen on any dashboard page. Clicking this arrow will open or collapse the side menu. This sidebar contains options, settings, and topic choices to customize your learning.

Step 3: Choose Your Level

Using the opened side menu on the Reading or Listening dashboard, select your CEFR target level (A1, A2, B1, or B2). Your choice will be synced automatically across all devices!

Step 4: Practice Daily Core Skills

  • Input (Lesen & Hören): Generate a reading text or listening audio. Try to understand the context first without translating everything. Then, answer the comprehension questions.
  • Output (Schreiben & Sprechen): Choose a topic or prompt. When writing, focus on grammar and let the AI grade you. When speaking, use the mic to record your sentence and instantly see if you were grammatically correct and on-topic!

Step 5: Use the Dictionary Widget

Stuck on a word? Don't leave the app! Click the 📚 floating dictionary icon in the bottom right of any page. Type in an English or German word to get translations and verb conjugations instantly.
